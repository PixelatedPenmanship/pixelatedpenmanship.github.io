## The Philosophy: Ink × Silicon

In an era saturated by disposable software, ephemeral notifications, and synthetic text, **Pixelated Penmanship** exists as an intentional counterbalance. 

By day, I explore **systems engineering, embedded cybersecurity, and resilient computing environments**—working with low-level Linux primitives, hardware roots of trust (TPM 2.0, ARM TrustZone), and safety-critical standards. 

Off-screen, I study **mechanical writing instruments**—fountain pens, capillary feed dynamics, filling architectures, and archival ink formulation. The common thread is **craftsmanship**: whether writing a bare-metal driver or tuning a gold nib on micro-mesh, true quality requires discipline, patience, and attention to tactile detail.
